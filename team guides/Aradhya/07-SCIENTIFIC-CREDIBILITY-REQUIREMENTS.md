# Aradhya: truthful science in the interface and passports

Mandatory references: [architecture33](../../architecture%20files/33-SCIENTIFIC-CREDIBILITY-AND-CLAIM-GATES.md), [shared credibility gate](../../team%20coordination/06-SCIENTIFIC-CREDIBILITY-AND-RELEASE-GATE.md), architecture11/16–19/26/29 and [assurance schema](../../architecture%20files/contracts/scientific-assurance.schema.json). Your interface is part of scientific correctness: a hidden unknown or misleading summary invalidates an otherwise careful backend result.

## U0 types and display contract

Generate the client from accepted C1/F0 schemas, including required `AnalogPassport.assurance_evidence_id`. Consume audited claims/evidence through existing API endpoints and Tanush's resolved renderer bundle. Do not invent scientific confidence percentages, compute qualification in the browser, reinterpret coverage denominators or generate a competing science summary with an LLM. Missing audit/field/data stays unavailable or incomplete; never optimistic PASS.

Separate qualification PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL/GAP/UNKNOWN, verification PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE, claim disposition, audit publication ALLOWED/LIMITED/BLOCKED and workflow completion. A completed investigation or an ALLOWED negative passport is not mission success. A limited report can be useful without a green universal recommendation.

Every result row supports inspection of requirement/rule, measured quantity/units, Earth/target scope, native/effective support, coverage/quality, proxy/direct status, uncertainty or unquantified limitation, assumptions, evidence IDs, actual validation level and mode. Use progressive disclosure for detail; keep essential unknowns/failures/GAP and materially fragile conclusions visible in the summary. Keep status text/icons beyond color.

## Maps and evidence

Show layer body/frame, native/effective scale, masks, legend and attribution. Smooth imagery is a display treatment, not a new measurement. Upsampling cannot change a source's information support. Coarse terrain supporting regional context cannot be labeled verified wheel clearance, traction, sampling or autonomous navigation. Moon/Mars frames must not be rendered as Earth locations. WebGL failure retains equivalent table/evidence access.

The evidence drawer resolves real registered IDs, version/algorithm/parameter hashes and source lineage. Label synthetic, replay and live on screens and exports, including replay of actual NASA data. Show related-source dependence where material. Separate measurement uncertainty, model sensitivity and qualitative evidence strength; HIGH is not a probability. Unassessed confidence cannot become a decorative percentage.

A source access error is not a scientific FAIL. Missing thermal/mineral coverage is UNKNOWN, with an actionable explanation. Unresolved critic findings stay linked to the impacted requirement and its current restriction. No override button can waive a scientific defect; accepting an assumption has precisely the scope returned by the backend.

## U1 pure renderer and final claims

Render exact audited permitted statements and preserve their scope; no “Mars ready”, certified safety, globally novel discovery or physically proven reserve. Display bounded NOT_IN_REFERENCE_LIST claims with the declared reference/search scope. Every essential failed/unknown requirement, accepted proxy, natural GAP and calibration limitation survives HTML/JSON/CSV/export/reload. Counts show their full denominator and separate unknown/partial/gap treatment.

Use the required assurance evidence pointer/digest in the final report. The pure renderer accepts a fully resolved immutable bundle without DB/model/network. Refuse a contradictory/incomplete final bundle with a typed renderer error; draft previews may show a clearly labeled incomplete report. Do not independently upgrade or rewrite the backend publication decision. Preserve all attempts, initial failures, actual repairs and unchanged accepted input hashes.
