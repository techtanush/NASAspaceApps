At each handoff give the integrator commit SHA, merged base, dependency requests, exact runnable commands, observed output and remaining limitations. A brief verbal "done" without a reproducible result is not a handoff.

If behind, preserve the deterministic terrain-first end-to-end loop before adding expressive visuals or extra regions. Optional scientific layers keep tested no-coverage states. Any deliberate scope change is recorded, not silently marked complete. Do not spend the final hour merging three independently scaffolded applications.

Local-only work does not require hosting/publication. Do not consume hackathon time integrating sponsors or hardware stacks excluded by the architecture. If the actual event duration differs, preserve checkpoint dependencies and compress presentation/stretch work rather than deleting integrity checks.
