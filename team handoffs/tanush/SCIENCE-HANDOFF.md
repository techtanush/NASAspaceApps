# Tanush platform science handoff

This branch implements scientific claim enforcement, evidence registration boundaries, immutable passport assembly and export orchestration. Its observed tests establish software behavior with typed synthetic inputs. They do not establish terrain, rover, energy, source-decoder, uncertainty or analogue science correctness.

The [validation report](scientific-validation-report.json) leaves every full SCI-01–SCI-18 acceptance row **NOT_RUN** and records final source `eb4cd4c63e4477a050370c8084c89c596e9ea1af`. The declared S0 platform software scope is verified; its nested `platform_part` results describe only observed guards. S1–S4 remain NOT_RUN and S5 remains NOT_IMPLEMENTED. No acquired NASA asset, real scientific receipt, physical experiment or external live-model receipt is claimed.
